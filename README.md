## ✨ Features

- **🎯 Futuristic Cyberpunk Webcam Viewfinder**:
  - Live video stream with real-time HUD overlays, crosshair reticle, corner brackets, and scanning laser line.
  - Camera flip (front/back), freeze/pause feed, and photo upload fallback.
  - Privacy-first: frames are processed on-the-fly and never permanently stored.

- **🔥 The Big "Roast Me 🔥" Button**:
  - Tactile, pulsing fiery button with red ember glow animations.
  - Rotating 18+ progress steps during visual analysis ("Checking your zero rizz...", "Smelling your room through the screen...", "Calculating how fast your ex ran...").
  - Shutter flash effect and flame burst audio.

- **💬 The Sarcastic 18+ Roast Display Box**:
  - Typewriter animation as the roast reveals.
  - Damage Rating Meter (e.g., `Emotional Damage: 99%`, `Kitchen Nightmare: 100%`).
  - Humorous 18+ categorization tags (`#ZeroRizzAlert`, `#LeftOnRead`, `#WalkingHangover`, `#SecretGroupChat`).
  - **🔊 Read Aloud**: Built-in SpeechSynthesis voice with deadpan sarcastic delivery.
  - **📋 Copy Roast**: One-click copy with instant toast confirmation.
  - **🎉 Canvas Confetti**: Custom firework ember particles on every burn.

- **🎭 6 Comedic Personas**:
  1. **🔥 18+ Savage**: Brutal, unfiltered comedy club roast.
  2. **👨‍🍳 Chef Ramsay**: Loud, furious Gordon style screaming at your face & room.
  3. **💼 Toxic Boss**: Uncensored performance review destroying your career prospects.
  4. **💻 Gamer / Nerd**: Zero rizz, virgin energy & touch grass burns.
  5. **🌌 Depressed AI**: Dark crisis about your life choices and wasted youth.
  6. **🍻 Trash Talk**: Savage 2 AM bar banter between homies.

- **🧠 Dual AI Vision Engine**:
  - **Online**: Connect your free Google Gemini API key (`gemini-2.0-flash` vision model) for live multimodal visual inspection of your room, clothing, posture, and facial expression.
  - **Offline/Built-in**: Works immediately out of the box with zero setup using an intelligent comedic roast matrix.

- **📜 Hall of Flame (Roast History)**:
  - Slide-out drawer capturing session roasts with snapshots, damage ratings, and copy tools.
